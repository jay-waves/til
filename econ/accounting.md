## The Accounting Equation 

$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owners' Equity}$$

- **Assets**: All of property owned by the company
- **Liabilities**: All of debts that the company currently has outstanding to lenders. 
- **Sharedholders' Equity**: company's ownership interest in assets, after all debts have been repaid

企业资产是债权人的钱和企业所有者的钱之和。

*Current assets (Liabilities)* are those that are expected to be converted into cash within 12 months or less.
Everything that isn't a current assets is, *a long-term asset*.
Some Balance over a period of time is split up on the sheet, so the next 12m' payments are shown 
as a current asset (Liability), while the remainders are shown as a long-term one.

## DCF (Discounted Cash FLow) 

$$P=\sum^T_{t=1} \frac{C F_t}{(1+r)^t}$$

把一项资产未来 $t$ 年能产生的现金流 $CF_i$ ，通过折现率 $r$ ，折现为资产今天的价值 $P$.
